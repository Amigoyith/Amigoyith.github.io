## The flight computer

During ascent a rocket can pull enough g's to saturate a typical accelerometer, and that's exactly when you need a good estimate of where the rocket is. I'm writing the club's first active-control flight computer driver in embedded C. It combines redundant sensors so the state estimate stays reliable through high-g saturation, which cut our apogee-detection timing error by about 30%. I also ran a magnetometer interference study.

## Teaching

I wrote a Level 1 rocket design guide for beginners and have led rocket-building sessions for more than 60 students.

## Competitions

The team placed 5th, 3rd and then 1st at Friends of Amateur Rocketry Unlimited contests in 2024, 2025 and 2026.
