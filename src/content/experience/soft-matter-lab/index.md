## The research

Latch-mediated spring-actuated systems store elastic energy and release it very quickly. They show up in nature, in things like the strikes of mantis shrimp and trap-jaw ants. Our lab studied the physics behind them through experiments and simulation.

## My contribution

I automated the lab's recoil-motion analysis in MATLAB: the Computer Vision Toolbox tracks the material's position frame by frame in high-speed video, and I smooth that position data to compute velocities and characterize energy efficiency.

That work went into a co-authored poster, *Measuring the High-Rate Large Deformation Recoil of Elastic Materials*, presented at the SoCal Soft Matter Symposium and the APS Physics Global Summit.
